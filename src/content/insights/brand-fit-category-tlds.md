`.com` remains the default assumption for many buyers, but construction and trade brands increasingly win with category TLDs when the name itself is exact and memorable.

## When `.construction` is the better fit

- Your primary audience already thinks in trade language: GC, builder, remodeler, developer.
- The second-level name is short enough to print on a truck door without truncation.
- You want search snippets and ad paths to reinforce category relevance immediately.
- Local SEO is a growth channel and the domain mirrors city + service intent.

## When to keep hunting for `.com`

- You need a multi-vertical consumer brand where “construction” would feel too narrow.
- Legal or franchise guidelines require a specific legacy TLD.
- The available `.construction` option is long, hyphenated, or hard to spell aloud.

## Practical decision test

Say the domain once to a superintendent and once to a homeowner. If both parties understand what you do without explanation, brand fit is strong. **ABQ.Construction** passes that test for Albuquerque-focused builders: city code, category TLD, and zero ambiguity about the market.
